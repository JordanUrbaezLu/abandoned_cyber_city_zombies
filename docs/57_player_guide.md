# Abandoned Cyber City — New Player Guide

*A quick, complete guide for anyone jumping into the map for the first time. Read it in
five minutes, then go play. Numbers here track the actual code — if something feels off,
the code wins.*

---

## The 60-second version

- You have **two currencies.** **Points** (cash) keep you alive *this* round — guns, doors,
  perks, Pack-a-Punch. **Data Shards** are the rare upgrade currency, earned and spent
  **underground in the trench**, and they make you permanently stronger.
- **Guns** come from the wall and the **Mystery Box**. The better a gun is, the **rarer**
  it rolls and the **more it costs to Pack-a-Punch**.
- **Pack-a-Punch has 3 levels** (I → II → III). Each level costs more for stronger guns.
- The **trench** (underground) is the high-risk, high-reward heart of the map: amped
  zombies, but it's the only place to earn **Data Shards**.
- Spend Shards on **perk slots**, the **Exo Suit** (your body), the **Weapon Overclock**
  (your gun), and the **Glitch Altar** gamble.

**Core loop:** survive up top for cash → drop into the trench for Shards → spend Shards on
permanent power → come back able to go deeper → repeat.

## Guns: tiers, Pack-a-Punch cost, and box odds

Every gun is rated **Base** (off the wall / box) and **Packed** (fully Pack-a-Punched).
The stronger a gun is when packed, the **rarer it rolls in the box** and the **more each
Pack-a-Punch level costs.**

**Tiers:** **S** = best, then **A**, **B**, **C**.

### Special weapons (box only)

| Weapon | What it is | PaP cost (I / II / III) | Box roll |
|---|---|---|--:|
| **Thundergun** | Wonder weapon — wind blast clears a room. One per game, the rarest pull. | 5000 / 7500 / 10000 | ~0.6% |
| **Mahem** | Explosive rocket launcher. | 4000 / 6000 / 8000 | ~5.9% |
| **Action Figure** | Melee special — Pack-a-Punches **in place** (no swap), +1 cleave target per level. | 5000 / 7500 / 10000 | ~1.0% |

> **Reading the odds:** percentages are the gun's share of the box. The box never hands you
> a gun you're already holding, so as you collect, the remaining guns come up more often.
> Equipment (Monkey Bomb, etc.) can also appear, which slightly lowers each gun's real
> chance. **Best guns are deliberately rare** — that rare S-tier roll is supposed to feel like
> a win.

## Data Shards — the upgrade currency

Shards are **separate from points** and are **only earned underground**. Cap **500**.
You spend them on the four upgrade systems below.

**Where Shards come from (all in the trench):**

| Source | Where | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Caches** (×2) | The open pit | **+3 each**, once per round (first looter gets it; in co-op you can only loot **one of the two** — the other is for a teammate). A dim white glow means "not looted yet this round." |
| **Trench Warden** (a Brutus boss) | Near the trench | **+3 to the whole team** on most kills. |
| **Reactor Surge** | Pit (north) | Activate it, survive a tense **5-wave** onslaught (fast spawns + armored elites — think twice before starting it) → **+5 to everyone + a shared Insta-Kill.** Re-arms every few rounds. |
| **Glitch Altar** | Foundry room | A gamble, not a faucet (see below). |

## The Trench — how it works

The **trench** is the underground danger zone. It's where Data Shards live, so it's where
the map's depth comes from.

**Getting down there:**
- Buy into the **Bus Station** (corp zone) — two doors, **1000 each**, no power needed.
- **Drop into the open pit** (take the stairs, or just jump in). Jumping in costs a tiny
  **25-damage fall tax** (PhD Flopper cancels it) — it won't kill a healthy player.

**Why it's dangerous — the deeper, the worse.** The trench goes down in **layers**. Every
layer deeper, zombies get **+5% faster, +50% more health, and hit for +10 more** than the
layer above. While you're underground you also move **slower** (see the Exo Suit), and a
**spawn surge erupts at your current layer** whenever you arrive or descend — so the deep
floors fill up as you reach them.

**Why it's worth it.** Everything that makes you *permanently* stronger is bought down here:

| Thing | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Neural Expansion Bay** | Pit (west) | Buy **extra perk slots** (start with 4, up to 10). |
| **Exo Suit station** | Pit (east) | Upgrade your **body** — see below. |
| **Weapon Overclock terminal** | Foundry room | Upgrade the **gun you're holding** — see below. |
| **Glitch Altar** | Foundry room | **Gamble 2 Shards a spin** for a weighted result — usually a boon (free perk, Insta-Kill, Max Ammo, Double Points, a Shard jackpot, rare big win), sometimes a curse (a surge, a small Shard drain, or a dud). It's net-negative on purpose — you can't farm it. |

**Perk slots** (Neural Expansion Bay) cost **4 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 14** Shards for each slot
from 5th to 10th.

**The trench loop:** descend → loot the caches → spend at the Foundry and the Bay → arm the
Reactor for the big payout → come back stronger and push a layer deeper.

## The Exo Suit — your body upgrade

**The why:** the trench's deep layers are where the best Shards are, but the deeper you go
the more the trench **slows you down**. The Exo Suit is the *only* thing that cancels that
slow — so it's the key that unlocks the deep layers. It also toughens you and turns your
knife into a real weapon.

**Per player. 10 tiers.** Bought at the Exo Station in the pit. Your HUD shows **EXO SUIT
N/10**. Three things scale with your tier:

| Augment | Each tier | At Tier 5 | At Tier 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Walk deeper at full speed** | +1 layer | normal speed through layers 1–5 | (5 layers built today) |
| **Damage resistance** | −5% | −25% | **−50%** |
| **Melee damage** | +30% | +150% | **+300%** |

**The depth slow it cancels** — at any layer *deeper* than your Exo tier you move slower
(−20% at the first uncovered layer, then −10% per layer below):

| Your tier ↓ / Layer → | L1 | L2 | L3 | L4 | L5 |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| **0** | −20% | −30% | −40% | −50% | −60% |
| **2** | 0 | 0 | −20% | −30% | −40% |
| **4** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | −20% |
| **5+** | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

> The map has **5 built layers** today, so the depth gate maxes out at Tier 5. Tiers 6–10
> still keep adding resistance and melee.

**Cost:** **4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32 / 36 / 40** Shards per tier (= 4 × tier; **220** to fully max).

## The Weapon Overclock — your gun upgrade

**The why:** Pack-a-Punch is a one-time power jump; the Overclock is **permanent, per-gun
scaling** that keeps a favorite weapon relevant deep into a run. It's the gun counterpart to
the Exo Suit — same Shard cost, same "grow it over time" idea.

**Per gun** (the one you're holding; it sticks through Pack-a-Punch). **10 tiers**, bought at
the Overclock terminal in the Foundry. Your HUD shows the held gun's tier as **vN**. **Every
weapon can be overclocked except the Action Figure.** Each tier nudges up four effects:

| Effect | At Tier 5 | At Tier 10 (max) |
|---|---|---|
| **Flat damage** — all hits | +50% | **+100%** |
| **Glitch piercing** — bonus vs glitch zombies | +125% | **+250%** |
| **Ammo refund** — chance a headshot kill returns a round | 50% | **100%** |
| **Shield piercing** — vs the Riot enemy's front armor | full bypass | **front becomes a weak point (bonus damage)** |

> The Overclock changes **damage and on-hit behavior** — it does *not* touch fire rate,
> reload, or mobility (those are baked into the weapon).

**Cost:** **4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32 / 36 / 40** Shards per tier (= 4 × tier; **220** to fully max one gun).

## Quick-start checklist

1. Grab a wall gun and open toward the **Bus Station** (corp zone).
2. Buy into the trench (1000 a door) and **drop into the pit**.
3. **Loot the two Data Caches** every round; kill the **Trench Warden** when he shows.
4. Spend your first Shards on **perk slots** (Neural Expansion Bay) and an early **Exo Suit**
   tier so the trench stops slowing you.
5. Find a gun you like, **Pack-a-Punch** it, then **Overclock** it as Shards come in.
6. Arm the **Reactor** for a big Shard + Insta-Kill payout, push a layer deeper, repeat.

*Good luck out there.*
